# Problem statement

Finance, project, hiring, skills, AI adoption, and infrastructure planning often live in separate systems. Delayed work and changing demand can therefore appear as a workforce problem only after options have narrowed.

WRI targets workforce planning, finance, delivery, and platform leaders. Its hypothesis is that an evidence-linked aggregate signal, paired with reversible scenarios and human review, can create earlier options without turning workforce software into an employee-ranking tool. Measurable outcomes for a real deployment would include alert lead time, forecast error, review completion, and percentage of recommendations with evidence and uncertainty.
